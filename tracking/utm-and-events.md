# Tracking Budget Reset

## UTM
- Instagram Reel: `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=leadgen&utm_content=<slug>
- TikTok: `utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=leadgen&utm_content=<slug>`
- YouTube Shorts: `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=leadgen&utm_content=<slug>`
- Bio: `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=always_on&utm_content=profile`

## Event taxonomy
`page_view`, `generate_lead`, `start_budget`, `budget_completed`, `goal_created`, `offer_view`, `checkout_started`, `purchase`.

Il CSV dei Reel contiene uno slug UTM Content univoco per ogni contenuto.
